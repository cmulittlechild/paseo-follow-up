- Catch up: Home's "Catch up · N" (or ⌘K "Follow-up: Catch up on what needs you") walks everything that needs you one card at a time, in Home's order. Answer, merge or sign off and the next card is there; Skip brings a card round again at the end, Later snoozes it. More context asks the Overseer or the card's agent ("I don't get it" is one tap) and the deck moves on while it waits; that workspace comes back as your next card with the answer on it. On a desktop the queue sits in a resizable rail beside the card.
